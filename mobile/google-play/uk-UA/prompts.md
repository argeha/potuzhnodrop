## Обкладинка Google Play

Use case: ads-marketing / compositing.
Asset type: Google Play feature graphic, landscape 1024 × 500 pixels, opaque RGB.
Primary request: Create a premium, distinctive promotional cover for the Ukrainian
virtual collection parody game ПОТУЖНО DROP, matching its established dark navy,
warm gold, electric blue and restrained violet art direction. This is promotional
art, not a gameplay screenshot or a new interface design.
Input images: awp-king-v3.png and knife-club-v3.png are existing in-game case
illustrations, supporting product-art references. Preserve their ornate blue/gold
and purple/gold case identity. Use the case and collectible aesthetic, not any
third-party trademarks.
Scene: rich midnight-blue studio space, subtle metallic planes, thin warm-gold
light trails and a very restrained electric-blue aura. No casino imagery.
Subjects: one exquisite blue/gold open collectible case with its ornate blue
sniper-style digital collectible and a smaller purple/gold knife collectible case.
Both clearly read as digital collection art, not a first-person shooter scene.
Composition: sophisticated editorial composition, title in the left-center,
hero collectibles in the right-center, generous clean space and crop-safe margins.
All important content within the middle 80% of width and 76% of height. No huge
duplicated app-icon emblem. No device frames. Background extends to every edge.
Lighting: controlled cinematic gold rim light, beautiful material definition,
clean elegant shadows, strong subject hierarchy, no explosion of particles.
Text verbatim: "ПОТУЖНО DROP" as a bold perfectly legible Ukrainian Cyrillic
wordmark in two lines; below, "Кейси. Апгрейд. Колекція."; small but legible footer
"Ігрова пародія • Усе віртуальне". Render this spelling exactly, no extra text.
Avoid: Google Play badges, Steam/Valve/CS2 logos, ranking or awards, install/play
calls to action, prices, money, coins raining, gambling imagery, real-world prizes,
fictional game UI, hands, characters, gore, excessive neon, microtext, watermarks.
Output: a single wide finished cover at approximately 2.048:1 aspect ratio,
with clean safe margins for the exact 1024 × 500 export.
